## 1. Governance Clarification: Confirmatory Gate vs. Restricted Volatility-Risk Serving

A critical architectural distinction required by Section 2 and Section 4 of the PRD is the separation between:
1. **Restricted Volatility-Risk Serving Authorization (`AUTHORIZED_FOR_VOLATILITY_RISK_ONLY`):**
   - Applies solely to `HAR-RS-DOW-v1.0` for 168-hour annualized realized variance forecasting (`rv7d_var_ann`).
   - Requires: Verified SHA-256 artifact hash, verified model coefficients, 10-feature schema match, non-empty closed candle history, valid C2 conformal envelope $[L_t, U_t]$, and passing all 9 startup gates (`validation/startup_gate.py`).
   - Does NOT permit directional price forecasting, trade execution, or broker connections.
2. **Confirmatory Research Prospective Gate (`CONFIRMATORY_PROSPECTIVE_GATE_SATISFIED`):**
   - Requires $\ge 100$ clean, non-overlapping post-freeze prospective blocks accumulated in the append-only ledger (`data/prospective_forecast_ledger.jsonl`).
   - Tracked separately per horizon:
     - **24-Hour Horizon (`rv24h_var_ann`):** Currently at **5 / 100** raw resolved blocks, **1 / 100** strictly non-overlapping clean blocks (Requires ~100 calendar days).
     - **168-Hour Horizon (`rv7d_var_ann`):** Currently at **0 / 100** resolved clean blocks (4 pending; Requires ~700 calendar days).
   - Status is strictly `COLLECTING` until the 100-block threshold is satisfied and verified via the Diebold-Mariano test with Harvey-Leybourne-Newbold (HLN) finite-sample correction against the Student's $t_{T-1}$ reference distribution.
3. **Directional Return Model Authorization (`DIRECTIONAL_MODEL_AUTHORIZATION`):**
   - Status: `OFFLINE_RESEARCH_ONLY`.
   - The 90% directional hit rate remains an **unsupported research hypothesis** on unconditional BTC/USD returns. Directional models are never authorized for production serving.
4. **Live Trading & Capital Execution:**
   - Status: `DISABLED` (Permanent Fail-Closed Invariant across all endpoints, configuration, and interfaces).
