# 📜 BTCUSD Model Claims & Boundary Contract

## 1. What BTCognitive Explicitly Claims

1. **Probabilistic BTCUSD Range Forecasting**: Emits calibrated 24-hour price boundaries ($P_{10}, P_{50}, P_{90}$) with empirical **90.32% joint path containment**.
2. **Excursion Magnitude Estimation**: Emits quantified Maximum Favorable Excursion (MFE) and Maximum Adverse Excursion (MAE) quantiles.
3. **Monotonic Uncertainty Calibration**: Quantifies forecast dispersion where higher predicted uncertainty directly correlates with higher realized market dispersion.
4. **Statistical Challenger Governance**: Enforces formal 8-point promotion gates and paired permutation tests before any model replacement.

---

## 2. What BTCognitive Explicitly Does NOT Claim

1. **NO Guaranteed Directional Prediction**: Directional overlays are experimental secondary research signals and do NOT guarantee price trajectory.
2. **NO Validated Trading Alpha**: Previous sizing forensics established that point-in-time trading strategies do not possess positive alpha after realistic trading friction.
3. **NO Real Automated Trading**: BTCognitive does not place live orders or manage real capital.
4. **NO Universal Forecasting Superiority**: Validated empirical superiority is established strictly against baseline alternatives (EWMA, ATR, Historical Percentiles) on 31 independent 24h blocks.
