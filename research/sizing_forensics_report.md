| Variable Name | Source / Formula | Available at Decision Time t? | Leakage Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Root Cause Analysis

- **Finding**: `In previous research, 'score_b' was computed using the actual future target array 'mfe_conf = mfe_long[val_end_idx:]' rather than the model prediction 'pred_mfe_conf'. This caused the position-sizing weight to unknowingly trade larger on future large-winning bars and smaller on future adverse bars, producing an artificially inflated Sharpe (+46.71).`
- **Correction**: `Replaced 'score_b' input with strictly predicted values 'pred_mfe_conf' and 'pred_mae_conf' generated by models trained strictly on the training partition.`
