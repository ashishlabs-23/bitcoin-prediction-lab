# CoinMarketCap Basic rights questionnaire — do not activate before written answer

Please confirm, for the named BTCognitive legal entity and current Basic plan, whether each item is permitted and cite the controlling agreement section:

1. Public display of BTC candles in a browser charting/analytics site; whether it is view-only or may be subscription/ad supported.
2. The precise instrument semantics for each approved endpoint: aggregate/reference or venue-specific; pair, quote currency, OHLCV methodology, timezone, source timestamp and revisions.
3. Exact Basic-plan intraday OHLCV resolutions, history/backfill depth, update delay, monthly credits, rate limit and user/concurrency constraints. Reconcile any pricing/documentation differences.
4. Server-side cache, historical retention, encrypted backups, post-cancellation retention and disaster recovery.
5. Conventional indicators, derived statistics, research reports, forecast/risk-envelope displays and attribution wording.
6. Separate permissions for data as model inference input, training, tuning, calibration, validation, benchmarking and backtesting.
7. Whether user raw-data export, a customer-facing candle API, chart screenshots or downloadable reports are allowed. BTCognitive's default proposed scope is view-only, no candle export/API.
8. Geographic scope, future monetisation, data processors/subprocessors, API-key storage requirements, audit/termination obligations and any additional data-provider pass-through conditions.

Until CoinMarketCap answers each relevant item in writing, its Basic plan is a candidate only; it is not a production or public-provider approval.
