## Minimum written confirmation request

For a named legal entity and named plan/agreement, ask whether BTCognitive may: display candles to public users; sell a subscription around that display; retain/cache/back up raw and normalized bars; compute and display indicators, analytics, research and forecasts; use the data for model input, training, tuning, validation and backtesting; deliver any raw or reusable data to users; and operate in intended geographies. The answer must identify attribution, source/venue semantics, history, granularity, rate limits, SLA, termination, and deletion/retention obligations.
