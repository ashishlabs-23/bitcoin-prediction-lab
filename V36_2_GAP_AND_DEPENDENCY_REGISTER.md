## Dependency order for the next legitimate work

1. **Resolve lineage and frozen semantics:** accept or reject the 1m dataset’s source/timestamp interpretation against the signed-off V3 contract; identify whether the claimed holdout was ever isolated.
2. **Reconcile current code to contract:** decide the documented cost/fill interpretation and amend the existing research entry builder, detector, and canonical resolver only where frozen rules are demonstrably violated; add deterministic conformance tests.
3. **Re-establish reproducibility without protected writes:** design a non-mutating runner with immutable input/source/config identity and isolated output paths; reconcile historical reports before using any metrics.
4. **Freeze evaluation protocol:** chronological folds, purge/embargo, candidate-overlap rule, label-failure exclusions, costs, TP-first sensitivity, calibration separation, dependence-aware uncertainty, and multiple-testing controls.
5. **Only then generate/re-evaluate labels.** No new model training until the label/evaluation gates pass. Keep the product resolver and research resolver separate unless a reviewed compatibility contract proves equivalence.
